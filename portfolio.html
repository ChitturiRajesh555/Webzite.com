Our Work

A couple of recent projects — more will be added here as we go, but these give a real sense of the kind of work we do end-to-end, from the website itself through to the marketing that drives traffic to it.

K-INN Executive Stay - kinnstay.com

Industry: Hospitality / Co-living & PG accommodation

What they needed:

A professional website that could represent their co-living and executive stay spaces properly, plus a way to turn enquiries into actual bookings.

What we built:

The full website, along with ongoing digital and performance marketing to bring in qualified leads.

Result:

Higher traffic and noticeably better lead conversion, achieved on a leaner ad budget.

K Inn Leads | 22-04-2026 — Performance

CTR 1.41%
CPC ₹4.89
Frequency 2.00
Reach 50,365
  • CTR: 1.41% — a healthy click-through rate for a Leads-objective campaign, indicating the ad creative and targeting are resonating with the audience it's reaching.
  • CPC: ₹4.89 — a reasonably efficient cost per click, keeping acquisition cost low relative to spend.
  • Reach: 50,365 — a moderate-sized audience reached over the period.
  • Impressions: 100,949 — total ad views, giving a frequency of about 2.0 (each person saw the ad roughly twice on average), which is a healthy level of repeat exposure without tipping into overexposure/fatigue.
  • Clicks: 1,427 — total engagement generated from those impressions.

CBox Homes - cboxhomes.com

Industry: Real Estate / Prefabricated & container homes

What they needed:

A website to showcase their container home builds, backed by marketing that could bring in serious buyer enquiries rather than just traffic.

What we built:

Website design and development, plus digital and performance marketing focused on driving qualified leads.

Result:

Increased site traffic and higher conversions on a lower ad spend

CBox Leads Ad — Performance

CTR 2.55%
Spend ₹10,890
CTR trend +142.6%
Cost/result trend -39.5%
  • CTR at 2.55% actually clears the Lead Gen industry benchmark (~2.5–2.6%) — this ad is genuinely performing well.
  • ₹10.99 per messaging conversation is a strong cost per result — nearly 1,000 leads for under ₹11k spend.
  • This lines up with what we saw earlier: 142.6% CTR improvement trend and 39.5% cost-per-result improvement — this ad is your account's clear winner right now.

Overall: the numbers point to a well-targeted campaign with efficient spend and decent engagement — clicks are being generated at a reasonable cost without needing excessive reach or repeated exposure to drive action.
