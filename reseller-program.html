Reseller Program

White-Label Web Design & Marketing for Agencies

Run an agency or freelance and need extra hands without hiring? Webzyte builds websites and runs campaigns under your brand, so your client never knows we're involved.

How It Works

You bring the client relationship, we handle the delivery — design, development, or marketing execution — quietly, on your timeline, under your name.

Who This Is For

• Agencies overloaded during peak periods

• Freelancers who need design or dev support to take on bigger projects

• Marketing consultants who don't build websites themselves but need a reliable partner who does

What's Included

Everything we build for direct clients is available white-label — this isn't a stripped-down version for partners. That includes:

Website Design & Development

Responsive, mobile-friendly builds, typically delivered in 7–10 business days after content and approvals are in, with up to 2 rounds of design revisions included.

SEO & Digital Marketing Strategy

Keyword research, on-page and off-page optimization, content and blog optimization, with ongoing monthly monitoring and reporting.

Social Media Management

Account setup and branding, content calendars, regular posting, and monthly performance reports.

Meta Ads Management

Campaign setup, audience targeting, creative and budget optimization, monthly reporting (ad spend itself is billed separately by Meta).

Google Ads & Google Business Profile

GBP setup and optimization, campaign setup and management, keyword planning (ad spend billed separately by Google).
